ARCHITECTURAL PLANS AND Specifications

- Duly signed and sealed by a licensed Architect and approved by the owner/Applicant
- LOCATION PLAN within two kilometer radius for industrial and insitutional complex within a one kilometer radius for residential and commercial at any convenient scale showing important landmarks.
- SITE DEVELOPMENT PLAN at any scale showing position of the building within the lot also reflecting existing structures. It should also include the Technical description of the lot being applied. Setback of the building from property lines should be observed especially along the road right-of-way
- Certification from a licensed Geodetic Engineer that the proposed construction/renovation/repair shall not encroach any adjoining properties

BUILDING PERMIT APPLICATION FORM PROCESS:

1. DOWNLOAD THE FORM:
   - After logging in, users can download the Building Permit Application Form (PDF format)
   - Download link is available on the user dashboard
   - Click the "Download Application Form" button to save the PDF

2. FILL OUT THE FORM - TWO OPTIONS:

   OPTION A - DIGITAL FILLING:
   - Open the downloaded PDF in any PDF reader (Adobe Acrobat Reader, browser PDF viewer, etc.)
   - Click directly on form fields and type the required information
   - Required information includes:
     * Application Number
     * Owner/Applicant Full Name
     * Form of Ownership
     * For Construction Owned by an Enterprise
     * Address
     * Cellphone Number
     * Location of Construction
     * Scope of Work
     * Use/Character of Occupancy
   - After filling all fields, save the PDF to your computer 

   OPTION B - HANDWRITTEN FILLING:
   - Print the downloaded PDF form
   - Fill out all information manually using black or blue ink
   - Write clearly using capital letters for better legibility
   - After completing, scan the form or take a clear photo
   - Save as PDF, JPG, or PNG file on your computer

3. UPLOAD THE COMPLETED FORM:
   - Go back to your Konstructo dashboard
   - Navigate to "My Applications" or "Application Forms" section
   - Click the "Upload Completed Form" button
   - Select your file (PDF, JPG, JPEG, or PNG)
   - Ensure file size is within the 1 MB limit
   - Click "Submit" or "Upload" to send your form

4. ACCEPTED FILE FORMATS:
   - PDF files (.pdf) - Best for digitally filled forms
   - Images (.jpg, .jpeg, .png) - For scanned handwritten forms
   - Maximum file size: 1 MB

5. IMPORTANT REMINDERS:
   - Complete all required fields before submitting
   - You still need to visit the City Engineering Office for Box 2 for the wet signature of the Architect or Civil Engineer
   - Keep a copy of your completed form for personal records

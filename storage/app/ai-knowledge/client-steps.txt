After submission of complete requirements for building Permit

Agency Actions: 3 steps
1.1 Receives and check for completeness of the filled-up forms (incl. Ancillary Permit Form/s and Documentary Requirements)
1.2 Verification of documents (Simultaneous tasks of the LTO,LAO,LPDO)
1.3. Evaluation of Plans, specifications, and documents as to compliance to: Line and Grade Requirements, Architectural Requirements, Structural Requirements, Plumbing/Sanitary Requirements, Electrical Requirements, Mechanical Requirements, Electronics Requirements, Fire Safety Checklist (FSC) on Building Plans
1.4. Assess the fees to be paid and issues an OP and the Fire Code Construction Tax
1.5. Review OP, evaluates plans and signs OP together with the BFP OP
